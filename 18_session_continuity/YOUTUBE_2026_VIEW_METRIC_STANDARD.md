## 1. The Core Policy Change

Starting **August 24, 2026**, YouTube unified its view counting architecture across all video formats (Long-Form, Shorts, Live Streams, and Premieres):

| Metric Tier | Old Standard (Pre-August 2026) | New 2026 Standard (August 24, 2026+) |
|---|---|---|
| **Public View Count** | Required ~30 seconds of continuous watch time on long-form videos before registering on the public counter. | **Counts the moment a video begins to play — from the very first frame (0.1s)** across ALL video formats. |
| **Feed Autoplay** | Muted inline previews in mobile/desktop feeds did not increment long-form public views unless clicked. | **Muted auto-play in feeds registers as a public view immediately.** |
| **"Engaged Views"** | Implicitly combined with public views. | **Preserved as a separate analytical metric** in *YouTube Analytics > Advanced Mode* to track viewers who choose to continue watching past the opening threshold. |
| **Monetization & YPP** | Based on "valid public watch hours" and "valid public Shorts views". | **Strictly tied to "Engaged Views" and "Engaged Watch Hours" (Qualified Watch Hours)**. First-frame public views DO NOT count toward YPP thresholds or AdSense RPM payouts. |

## 3. The Operational Trap: Public Views vs Algorithmic Promotion

> [!WARNING]
> **NEVER CONFUSE PUBLIC VIEWS WITH ALGORITHMIC FUEL.**
> - **Public Views**: An outward-facing display metric measuring initial visual contact (first frame).
> - **Engaged Views & Average Percentage Viewed (APV)**: The true internal currency that drives YouTube's recommendation engine (Browse & Suggested feeds) and YouTube Partner Program (YPP) revenue.
> - If a video receives 10,000 first-frame public views but has low watch duration, YouTube's recommendation algorithm will immediately choke its distribution.
